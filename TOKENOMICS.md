# JARVIS tokenomics

The canonical tokenomics specification is maintained at
[docs/tokenomics.md](docs/tokenomics.md).

It defines the fixed 18.44 billion JARVIS supply, canonical Sui issuance,
bridge-backed Token-2022 representation on Solana, treasury and allocation
requirements, AI utility and credit separation, governance limits, reporting,
deployment conformance, and risk disclosures.
